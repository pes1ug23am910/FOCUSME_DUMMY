### 5. Known Bypass Limitations

FocusMe is designed to make bypassing website and app blocking difficult, but **no software can prevent all bypass methods**. Known bypass vectors include:

- Booting into Safe Mode (Windows, Android)
- Booting from USB/external media
- macOS Recovery Mode
- Using a device administrator account to uninstall the software
- Physical access to hardware (BIOS, bootloader)

These limitations are documented in our bypass test matrix. FocusMe provides the best commercially reasonable enforcement available through software.
